# Kwegatta 3-Minute Live Demo Script

**Event**: Hacktoberfest 2026 Hack Day Kampala x MUBS  
**Presenter**: The Kwegatta Team (Saifuddin Ahmed, Adinan Juuko, and team)  
**Total Target Time**: 3 minutes (180 seconds)

---

## 1. The Problem (0:00 – 0:30)

> *"Good morning, judges, mentors, and fellow builders. Look around this room at the Entrepreneurship, Innovation and Incubation Centre at MUBS today. In this room, we have brilliant business students who validated genuine market needs, sitting two desks away from skilled Flutter and Python developers who want to build something impactful. But they will spend all day here and never find each other. The same thing happens with learning: someone wanting to learn APIs sits next to someone who could teach it. Traditional social networks don't solve this because they optimize for followers, not complementary collaboration. That is why we built **Kwegatta**."*

---

## 2. Live Sign-Up & AI Profile (0:30 – 1:15)

> *(Presenter opens https://kwegatta.ai.studio on a phone or screen)*
>
> *"Joining Kwegatta takes about 2 minutes. Our conversational guide asks one question at a time: your name, what you offer, what you need, what you can teach, and what you want to learn. You can also paste your GitHub username.*
>
> *(Demonstrates typing: 'Amina Nakato', Offers: 'Market research, financial modeling, B2B sales', Needs: 'Flutter mobile developer')*
>
> *Watch what happens next: The open-weight model **gemma-4-26b-a4b-it** processes these facts on our server. It writes a crisp headline, a two-sentence first-person bio, tags, and role. Notice our hard constraint: **Gemma uses only the facts Amina provided**. It never invents degrees, awards, or fake companies. Amina can click 'Looks good' or ask Gemma to rewrite it."*

---

## 3. Complementary Matchmaking (1:15 – 2:00)

> *(Presenter clicks 'Looks good' and transitions to the Matches tab)*
>
> *"Here is where Kwegatta shines: **Complementary beats similar**. A developer looking for another developer is fine, but a business student who needs a mobile app matched with a mobile developer who needs customer acquisition is a startup in the making.*
>
> *Gemma 4 has evaluated the room and ranked the top 3 complementary matches. Look at this first match: Brian Okello, 94% match. Gemma tells us exactly why: 'Amina needs a mobile developer for a savings group app, which Brian specializes in with Flutter.' And Gemma goes one step further: it suggests a concrete project the pair could build right now: 'Kampala Student Thrift: mobile savings and lending circle app with automated ledger sync.' It even writes a friendly, warm WhatsApp icebreaker."*

---

## 4. Privacy-Preserving Connect (2:00 – 2:20)

> *(Presenter taps 'Connect')*
>
> *"In compliance with our Digital Public Goods alignment, Amina's phone number is never exposed to public crawlers. When Brian taps **Connect**, the system dispatches an in-app notification to Amina and opens WhatsApp with the pre-written message ready to send. In 10 seconds, two people in this room are actively collaborating on WhatsApp."*

---

## 5. Live Projector Wall & Learn Tab (2:20 – 2:40)

> *(Presenter switches to the `/wall` view on the projector)*
>
> *"For hackathon organizers, Kwegatta provides a dedicated Live Wall at `/wall`. Projected on the big screen, it displays live attendee counts, real-time match pairings, trending skills in the room, and a giant QR code so anyone walking into the auditorium joins instantly. And on the **Learn** tab, Kwegatta pairs every attendee with one peer mentor and one study partner so nobody leaves without learning a new skill."*

---

## 6. Open Source, DPG Alignment & What's Next (2:40 – 3:00)

> *"Kwegatta is 100% open source under the MIT License on GitHub. Every single AI feature is powered by the open-weight **Gemma 4 31B IT** model running on the server—no closed models, no browser API keys. The app includes full user data export in JSON and permanent profile deletion aligned with the Digital Public Goods Standard. Next, we are expanding Kwegatta to other campuses across Uganda and launching a WhatsApp bot bridge for offline access.*
>
> *Thank you, and happy building at Hacktoberfest 2026!"*
